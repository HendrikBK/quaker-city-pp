ABOUT THE BOOK

Author

George Lippard (1822-1854).

Publication date

1876 reprint of 1845 work.

Synopsis

This book, a combination of Gothic horror and urban crime thriller, was the bestselling novel in America prior to Uncle Tom's Cabin.

See this book's Wikipedia article for more information.
